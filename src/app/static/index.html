Top Discovery Insights

Q1 • WHY USERS STRUGGLE WITH OLD PHOTOS

Users face friction when relying on vague temporal or contextual queries to find old photos, as the search engine expects precise keywords or explicit metadata tags.

"I know I have a picture of my dog at the beach from 2019, but searching 'dog beach' just shows random sand photos."
15 reviews • Search Friction

Q2 • LOST ALBUM STRUCTURES

Album structures from legacy systems or physical transfers do not persist properly, leaving users with a massive chronologically disorganized feed.

"Transferred all my albums from my PC and now they are just one massive dump of photos by date. Impossible to find anything."
28 reviews • Data Migration

Q3 • RECEIPT & DOCUMENT RETRIEVAL

Searching for text within images (OCR) is perceived as inconsistent, especially for functional documents like receipts or screenshots.

"I took a photo of a receipt for a monitor I bought last month. Searching 'receipt' or 'monitor' brings up nothing."
12 reviews • OCR Failure

Q4 • FACE TAGGING LIMITATIONS

Users are frustrated when they cannot manually tag faces that the AI missed, preventing them from creating complete people albums.

"Google photos recognizes faces well most of the time, but when it misses one, there is no way for me to add a tag manually."
19 reviews • Face Tagging
